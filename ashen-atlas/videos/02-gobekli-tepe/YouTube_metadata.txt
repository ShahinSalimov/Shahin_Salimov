DESCRIPTION:
11,500 years ago, on a hill in southeastern Turkey, hunter-gatherers carved stone pillars up to 5.5 metres tall, decorated with foxes, snakes, boars and vultures. They had no metal, no writing and no farms. Then, over centuries, the great stone rings were filled with earth, and the hill kept its secret for ten thousand years.

In this documentary we explore Göbekli Tepe, one of the oldest monumental sites ever found: how it was discovered, what is carved on its pillars, who built it, and why it changes the story of how civilization began. We also separate the evidence from the myths, including "lost civilization" and comet theories.
