DESCRIPTION:
In September 1991, two hikers in the Ötztal Alps found a body melting out of the ice. They thought it was a lost climber. He had been dead for 5,300 years.

Ötzi the Iceman lived around 3300 BCE, in the Copper Age. He was about 45 years old, around 1.60 m (5'3") tall, with 61 tattoos, a copper axe, a bow he never finished and a stomach still full of his last meal. Ten years after his discovery, an X-ray revealed a stone arrowhead lodged in his left shoulder. Someone had shot him from behind.

Who was he? Why was he high in the mountains? And who wanted him dead? In this documentary we follow Ötzi through his final days, using the evidence of his body, his clothes, his tools and his DNA.
