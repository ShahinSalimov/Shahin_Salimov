Did a Chariot Kill Tutankhamun? #shorts

DESCRIPTION
Tutankhamun was buried with six chariots, the race cars of the Bronze Age. His mummy has a broken left leg, and some researchers have suggested a chariot accident. But he also had a damaged foot, and more than a hundred walking sticks were found in his tomb. Could he even drive one? Scholars still disagree.
