The Rockets in America's Anthem Started in India? #shorts

DESCRIPTION
In the 1780s and 1790s, the armies of Hyder Ali and his son Tipu Sultan of Mysore used iron-cased rockets against the British East India Company. After the fall of Seringapatam in 1799, the British captured hundreds of launchers and rockets and sent examples to England. William Congreve studied them at Woolwich and developed the Congreve rocket. In 1814 British ships fired Congreve rockets at Fort McHenry, the night Francis Scott Key described "the rockets' red glare".
Historians debate how much Congreve took from the Mysore design, but the link is widely accepted.
