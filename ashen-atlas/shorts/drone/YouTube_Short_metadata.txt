TITLE
The First Drone Attack Happened in 1849? #shorts

DESCRIPTION
In 1849 the Austrian army besieging Venice tried something new: balloons with no pilot, each carrying a bomb on a timed fuse. The idea is credited to artillery officer Franz von Uchatius. Some balloons were launched from the ship Vulcano. Then the wind turned, and some drifted back over the Austrian lines.
Sources disagree on how many balloons were launched (from two to about two hundred), but the raid is often called the first unmanned air attack in history.

Sources: Wikipedia (SMS Vulcano; Franz von Uchatius), History.com (This Day in History, 22 Aug 1849), Airminded.org (The first air bomb: Venice, 15 July 1849).
