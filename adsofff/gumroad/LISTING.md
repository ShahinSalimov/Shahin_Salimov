## Description (paste into the main description editor)

```
Stop staring at a blank page before every ad.

AdSofff turns a short product brief into a complete, production-ready ad campaign plan — for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and YouTube.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Describe your product, audience, the problem you solve and your call to action (4 short steps).
2. Get 5 genuinely different ad concepts — problem–solution, before & after, behind the scenes, myth-busting, social proof and more — each with a hook, angle, emotional trigger, message and visual direction.
3. Pick one and get the full campaign in one click.
4. Edit anything, then export a professional PDF report, a TXT script or a JSON project file.

WHAT YOU GET FOR EVERY CAMPAIGN
• 5 ad concepts adapted to your category, style and audience
• Timed video scripts for 15, 30, 45 or 60 seconds — scene durations always add up exactly
• Each scene: visual, camera direction, action, on-screen text, voiceover, sound effects, transition
• Storyboard studio: add, duplicate, delete, drag to reorder, timed preview
• Voiceover script with time codes and reading-time check per scene
• Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook captions, short title, hashtags and CTA ideas
• Copy written in English, Azerbaijani, Turkish or Russian
• Brand Kit: save your brand name, logo, colors, tone and audience
• PDF / TXT / JSON export and a print-friendly view

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• One file. No installation, no account, no subscription, no internet needed.
• Your campaigns stay in your browser on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
• Built-in backup and restore.

HONEST NOTES
• AdSofff builds content from a built-in library of proven ad structures. It is not an AI chatbot, and it labels its output clearly. Review and edit before you shoot.
• It plans your video. It does not record audio, render video or post to social media.
• Storyboard frames are simple sketches by shot type, not generated images.

WHO IT'S FOR
Social media managers, content creators, small business owners and agencies who need clear ad scripts and briefs fast — for their own brand or for clients.

WHAT'S IN THE DOWNLOAD
• AdSofff.html — the app (open in Edge or Chrome)
• START-HERE.html — 2-minute quick start (English and Azerbaijani)
• Sample Campaign — example PDF report, TXT script and JSON project
• LICENSE.txt

REQUIREMENTS
Windows, macOS or Linux with an up-to-date Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.

LICENSE
One license per person. Use it for your own business and for paid client work. Everything you create is yours.
```

## FAQ (add as part of the description or as a separate section)

```
Is this AI?
No. AdSofff uses a built-in library of proven ad structures and adapts them to your brief, category, style and language. It works offline, so nothing you type is sent to any AI service.

Do I need to install anything?
No. Unzip and double-click AdSofff.html. Use Microsoft Edge (built into Windows) or Google Chrome.

Where are my campaigns saved?
In your browser on your computer. Use Settings → Download backup to keep a copy or move to another computer.

Can I use it for client work?
Yes. Your license covers your own business and paid client work. You own everything you create.

Does it create the video or voice?
No. It writes the plan: concepts, scene-by-scene script, storyboard, voiceover text and captions. You (or your editor) shoot and record.

Does it work on Mac?
It is built to run in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on Mac as well as Windows. Safari and Firefox are not supported.

Which languages?
Hooks, voiceover, on-screen text and captions in English, Azerbaijani, Turkish or Russian. Camera and visual notes are in English.

Will I get updates?
Version 1.x updates are free through your Gumroad library.
```
