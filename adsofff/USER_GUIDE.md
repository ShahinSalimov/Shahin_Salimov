# User Guide

## 0. Try the sample
On the **Overview** page click **Open a sample campaign** (or **Add sample campaign** in My Campaigns). It creates a complete campaign for a fictional bakery so you can explore every screen and export.

## 1. Write the brief (New Campaign)
Four steps, with a progress bar at the top:

1. **Product** – name, category, description (20+ characters), main benefits (one per line), optional price. Optionally pick a **Brand Kit** profile to prefill details and tone.
2. **Audience** – target audience, the customer problem, the advertising goal.
3. **Format** – platform, duration (15/30/45/60 s), language, tone, style and call to action. Sensible defaults are preselected.
4. **Review** – check everything; **Edit** jumps back to a section. Then **Generate concepts**.

Fields are validated as you type and when you leave them. Your draft is saved in this browser automatically; **Discard draft** clears it. **Load example** fills a sample brief.

## 2. Choose a concept (Campaign Generator)
AdSofff drafts five concepts, each built on a different advertising structure (problem–solution, before & after, behind the scenes, myth-busting, comparison, social proof and more), adapted to your category and style.

- **View** – all fields: main idea, audience, angle, emotional trigger, hook, message, CTA, visual direction.
- **Edit** – change any field.
- **Regenerate** – replace one concept with an approach not already on screen.
- **★ Save** – keep a concept when you click **Regenerate unsaved**.
- **Create Full Campaign** – builds the script, storyboard, voiceover and social copy.

A yellow “Demo Generation · not AI” label means the content came from the local template engine.

## 3. Edit the script
The **Script** tab lists every scene: duration, shot type, visual, camera direction, action, on-screen text, voiceover, sound effects and transition. The timing bar warns when scenes no longer add up to the video length; **Fit to Ns** rebalances them. **Script length** regenerates the script for 15, 30, 45 or 60 seconds. Changes save automatically (“All changes saved”). Rename the campaign with the pencil next to its title.

## 4. Storyboard Studio
- Visual style: Sketch, Clean, Neon or Warm.
- Add, duplicate, delete and reorder scenes (drag cards or use the arrow buttons).
- **Edit** opens the full scene editor.
- **Preview storyboard** plays the scenes with their real timings (play, pause, previous, next).

Frames are simple illustrative sketches based on shot type — a planning aid, not generated images or video.

## 5. Voiceover Studio
- Time-coded script (`[0:00–0:04] line`) based on your scenes, fully editable.
- Estimated reading time vs. video length, plus a **per-scene fit** check.
- **Language** rewrites the script; **Tone** updates the delivery notes.
- **Regenerate** writes alternative lines; **Sync from script** rebuilds from the Script tab; **Copy** copies the text.

AdSofff does not create audio. Record with a voice artist or your own text-to-speech tool.

## 6. Social media content
Instagram caption, TikTok caption, YouTube Shorts description and Facebook text, plus hashtags (add/remove), a short title and CTA suggestions — all editable, each with a copy button. AdSofff does not post to social networks.

## 7. Export
On any campaign page: **PDF** (professional report: overview, concept, script and storyboard, voiceover, captions, hashtags), **TXT** (plain script), **JSON** (project file you can import again) and **Print view** (print or “Save as PDF” from the browser).

## 8. My Campaigns
Card or list view, search, platform filter and sorting. Each campaign can be **renamed**, **duplicated**, **exported as JSON**, **deleted** (with confirmation) and **opened**. **Import JSON** accepts project files and full backups.

## 9. Brand Kit
Save several brand profiles with logo (PNG/JPEG/WebP, max 500 KB), up to six colors, tone, audience, description, website and contact details, and reuse them in step 1 of a new campaign.

## 10. Settings
- **Generation engine** – shows whether an AI provider is available; AI is used only after you accept the data notice. You can turn it off at any time.
- **Backup and restore** – download everything as one JSON file, or restore from one (existing campaigns are not overwritten).
- **Privacy** – what is stored and where, plus **Delete all data**.

## Languages
Hooks, messages, on-screen text, voiceover and captions are written in the campaign language (English, Azerbaijani, Turkish, Russian). Production notes for the crew (visuals, camera, sound) stay in English. Text you type (benefits, problem, CTA) is used exactly as written.
