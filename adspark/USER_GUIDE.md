# User Guide

## 1. Create a campaign
1. Click **Create Campaign** (sidebar or top bar).
2. Fill in the brief. Required: name, category, description (20+ characters), main benefits, target audience, customer problem, goal, CTA, platform, duration, language, tone and style. Price is optional and is printed exactly as you type it.
3. Optional: pick a **Brand Kit** profile to prefill name, description, audience and tone.
4. Click **Generate concepts**. Your draft is kept while the tab is open; **Load example** fills a sample brief.

## 2. Choose a concept (Campaign Generator)
AdSpark drafts five concepts, each based on a different advertising structure (for example problem–solution, before & after, behind the scenes, myth-busting, comparison).
- **View** shows all fields: main idea, audience, angle, emotional trigger, hook, message, CTA and visual direction.
- **Edit** changes any field.
- **Regenerate** replaces one concept with a new approach not already on screen.
- **Star (Save)** keeps a concept when you click **Regenerate unsaved**.
- **Create Full Campaign** builds the script, storyboard, voiceover and social copy.

## 3. Edit the script
The **Script** tab lists every scene with duration, shot type, visual, camera direction, action, on-screen text, voiceover, sound effects and transition. The timing bar warns when scenes no longer add up to the chosen duration; **Fit to Ns** rebalances them proportionally. Changes save automatically (“All changes saved” in the top bar).

## 4. Storyboard Studio
- Pick a visual style: Sketch, Clean, Neon or Warm.
- Add, duplicate, delete and reorder scenes (arrow buttons, keyboard accessible).
- **Edit** opens the full scene editor.
- **Preview storyboard** plays the scenes with their real timings.
Frames are simple illustrative sketches based on shot type — a planning aid, not generated images.

## 5. Voiceover Studio
- Time-coded script (`[0:00–0:04] line`) based on your scenes.
- Estimated reading time vs. video length, with a warning when it is too long.
- Change **Language** to rewrite the script, **Tone** to update delivery notes.
- **Regenerate** writes alternative lines, **Sync from script** rebuilds from the Script tab, **Copy** copies the text.
AdSpark does not create audio. Record with a voice artist or your own TTS tool.

## 6. Social media content
Instagram caption, TikTok caption, YouTube Shorts description and Facebook text, plus hashtags (add/remove), a short title and CTA suggestions. Everything is editable and has a copy button.

## 7. Export
On any campaign page: **PDF** (report with concept, script, storyboard, voiceover, captions, hashtags), **TXT** (plain script), **JSON** (project file you can import again) and **Print view** (print or “Save as PDF” from the browser).

## 8. My Campaigns
Search, filter by platform, sort, **rename**, **duplicate**, **export JSON**, **delete** and **open**. **Import JSON** accepts project files and full backups.

## 9. Brand Kit
Save several brand profiles with logo (PNG/JPEG/WebP, max 500 KB), up to six colors, tone, audience, description, website and contact details.

## 10. Settings
- **Generation engine**: shows whether an AI provider is available; turn AI on only after reading the data notice.
- **Backup and restore**: download everything as one JSON file, or restore from one.
- **Privacy**: what is stored and where, plus **Delete all data**.

## Languages
Hooks, messages, on-screen text, voiceover and captions are written in the campaign language (English, Azerbaijani, Turkish, Russian). Production notes for the crew (visuals, camera, sound) stay in English. Text you type (benefits, problem) is used as written.
