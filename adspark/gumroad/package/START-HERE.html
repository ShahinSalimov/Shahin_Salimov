Open AdSpark.html to start. No installation, no account, no internet needed. Best in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.

Get started in 2 minutes

  1. Unzip the download to a folder you keep, for example Documents\AdSpark.
  2. Double-click AdSpark.html. If Windows asks which app to use, choose Edge or Chrome.
  3. Click Open a sample campaign to see a finished campaign: concepts, script, storyboard, voiceover and captions.
  4. Click Create Campaign, fill in the 4-step brief and generate your own five concepts.
  5. Pick a concept, click Create Full Campaign, edit anything, then export as PDF, TXT or JSON.

Your data and backups

Campaigns are saved automatically inside your browser on this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Use the same browser to see your campaigns again. Another browser starts empty.
  • Private/incognito windows delete data when closed, so use a normal window.
  • Clearing browser “site data” also deletes campaigns. Keep backups: Settings → Download backup. Restore with Restore from file.
  • Moving to a new computer: download a backup, copy it with AdSpark.html, then restore.

Good to know

  • How content is created: AdSpark uses a built-in library of proven ad structures (problem–solution, before & after, behind the scenes and more), adapted to your category, style, language and brief. It is not an AI model and the app labels it “Demo Generation · not AI”. Always review and edit before production.
  • Languages: hooks, voiceover, on-screen text and captions in English, Azerbaijani, Turkish or Russian. Production notes (camera, visuals) are in English.
  • Not included: audio/voice generation, video rendering, automatic posting to social networks.
  • Storyboard frames are simple sketches by shot type, a planning aid, not generated images.

Troubleshooting

Blank page or a storage warningOpen AdSpark.html in Edge or Chrome (not in a private window).
My campaigns are goneCheck you are in the same browser. Restore your latest backup in Settings.
PDF letters look wrongUpdate your browser to the latest version.
Something elseReply to your Gumroad purchase receipt email with a short description and a screenshot.

Məlumatlarınız və backup

Kampaniyalar bu kompüterdə brauzerinizin içində avtomatik saxlanılır. Heç nə internetə göndərilmir.
  • Kampaniyalarınızı görmək üçün eyni brauzerdən istifadə edin. Başqa brauzer boş açılır.
  • Gizli (incognito) pəncərə bağlananda məlumatları silir, adi pəncərədən istifadə edin.
  • Brauzerin “site data” təmizlənməsi kampaniyaları da silir. Backup saxlayın: Settings → Download backup. Bərpa: Restore from file.
  • Yeni kompüterə keçid: backup yükləyin, AdSpark.html ilə birlikdə köçürün və bərpa edin.
